AccessibleWay — Airport Route Integration

Connect the existing airport route candidate with sensor trajectory evidence. Route controls remain in the airport engine; motion evidence is a separate, exploratory observation stream.

Demonstration only. Airport connections, lifts and accessibility attributes are unverified. Motion evidence cannot establish a passenger's position, route adherence, step-free access or confidence. Do not use for live wayfinding, evacuation or assistance decisions.

What this integration does — and does not do

The original airport engine remains responsible for route computation and manual simulated waypoint progress. The sensor lab supplies variability, turning-like and stationary windows on a separate timeline. Associating the two records the operator's scenario choice only. It never advances the route from sensor observations, converts airport map units to metres or calculates probabilistic positioning confidence. A change of route invalidates the previous association.

4. Existing passenger experience

Mirrors the airport engine's route and manually simulated waypoint state. No sensor-derived position marker is introduced.
